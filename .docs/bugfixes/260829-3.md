# Bugfixes 260829-3

Split out of PR #558 (the `--cwd_matters` cleanup data-loss fix), where this was
found but did not belong: it touches no cleanup or path code, and kept 105 lines of
unrelated test diff in front of a reviewer checking a data-loss fix.

- [x] Subscription retry-budget test raced its own clock (was PR #558's LAYER 6): `TestSubscriptionReconnectResync` / "A permanently stopped manager closes only after reconnect retries are exhausted" (jobqueue/subscription_test.go:1557) failed once in a full `make test` with `So(subscriptionUpdatesStillOpen(sub, 75*time.Millisecond), ShouldBeTrue)` -> `Expected: true / Actual: false`. It passed 3/3 in isolation and on the immediately following full run. The assertion requires the subscription to still be open 75ms after the manager stops, against reconnect timings of 50ms/200ms, so under full-suite load the reconnect deadline can elapse inside the 75ms window. Unrelated to LAYERs 1-5 (no cleanup or path code involved). Fix it deterministically per testing-principles (control the clock or the reconnect scheduling, or observe the state transition rather than racing a wall-clock window) — do NOT loosen, skip or quarantine the check, which is guarding real reconnect-then-close behaviour from the 260724/260725 reliability work.
  - Red command (reproduces reliably): `for i in $(seq 8); do (while :; do :; done) & done; CGO_ENABLED=0 timeout 400 go test ./jobqueue/ -run 'TestSubscriptionReconnectResync' -count=6; kill %1 %2 %3 %4 %5 %6 %7 %8` — 8 busy loops on this 4-CPU box.
  - Red evidence (at commit 63cdd1a): `--- FAIL: TestSubscriptionReconnectResync (63.71s)`, `subscription_test.go:1557 Expected: true / Actual: false`. Also flaked unprompted in 2 of 3 full `make test` runs during LAYER 4.
  - Mechanism (read from the code, not guessed): the Convey sets `applySubscriptionReconnectTimings(&serverConfig, 50ms, 200ms)` — `retryWait` 50ms, `retryTime` 200ms — then `server.Stop`, `sub.closeSock()`, and asserts `subscriptionUpdatesStillOpen(sub, 75*time.Millisecond)`. `Subscription.reconnect` (jobqueue/subscription.go:354-378) sets `retryEnd = now + retryTime` when the poll error is noticed, retries `reconnectOnce` every `retryWait`, and calls `finish(ErrSubscriptionClosed)` once `time.Now().After(retryEnd)` — so the channel closes ~200ms after the poll error, on the subscription's own goroutine and clock. The assertion is an *absence* measured over a 75ms wall-clock window that starts in the test goroutine and is not synchronised with that budget, so a >=125ms deschedule of the test goroutine between `closeSock()` and the `select` makes the channel already closed when it is first read. `subscriptionUpdatesStillOpen` (subscription_test.go:2877) then returns the `ok` of a closed channel, i.e. false. Nothing is wrong with the product behaviour; the test races itself.
  - Fix direction (implementor decides): do NOT widen the window or loosen the check — the later `subscriptionErrBecomes(sub, ErrSubscriptionClosed, 3*time.Second)` assertion caps how large `retryTime` can be, so more slop only makes the race rarer. Prefer asserting the *invariant* the Convey is really about — "the subscription never closes before its retry budget expires" — by timestamping immediately before `closeSock()` and, if a close is observed, requiring that at least `retryTime` elapsed, which is an ordering claim rather than a scheduling one. Alternative: add a test seam counting reconnect attempts (there are no hooks in subscription.go today; `Client.liveTouchHook` at client.go:450 is the established in-package pattern) and assert the subscription survives at least one attempt before exhausting.
  - Fix (test-only; `jobqueue/subscription.go` untouched, md5-verified): the fixed-instant sample is replaced by an ordering claim — `wentAway := time.Now()` before `server.Stop`, then `subscriptionUpdatesClosedAfter(sub, wentAway, 3*time.Second)` returning `(elapsed, closed)`, asserting `closed` and `elapsed >= retryTime`. `subscriptionErrBecomes` deleted (no remaining callers; its 3s bound lives on as the helper's timeout, so a never-closing subscription still fails fast instead of hanging the suite). `So(sub.Err(), ShouldBeNil)` and `So(subscriptionUpdatesClosed(sub), ShouldBeTrue)` dropped as subsumed: `Subscription.err` is written only by `setErr` (unreachable here — ctx is Background and `Unsubscribe` is deferred past the assertions, and it passes nil anyway) and by `finish`, which sets the error and closes `updates` in the same `sync.Once`, so an early fatal error IS an early close; `finish(nil)` is still caught by the retained `errors.Is(sub.Err(), ErrSubscriptionClosed)`.
  - Second cause found and fixed in the same Convey: reconnect attempts are aimed at a dead port (`jq.args[0]`, via the existing `freeTestPort()`). Without it the Convey hung past its bound in 4 of 12 loaded runs, independently reproduced by the reviewer, because the manager's command socket still listens after it has killed every client subscription. That is a real product bug, recorded separately as item 12 — the test models around it rather than hiding it, and the comment says so.
  - `retryTime` raised 200ms -> 1s after a measurement disproved the reviewer's suggested alternative. The slack in the ordering claim is not "wentAway to the break" but "how long `server.Stop` blocks the test goroutine from reading the channel": measured 56-62ms loaded and unloaded, dominated by the fixed 50ms `ShutdownSocketWait` (server.go:6105). Moving `sub.closeSock()` before `Stop` therefore does NOT help — measured 61.59ms of slack with closeSock after Stop vs 62.40ms with it before — it just moves the same delay to the other side of the break. Raising the budget does help, because it shrinks the slack *relative* to the budget: 6.2% instead of 30.8%, moving the vacuity threshold (where the assertion could no longer fail at all) from `Stop >= 200ms` to `Stop >= 1s`, ~16x the measured value. The negative result is recorded in the comment so a future reader does not "tighten" it by reordering.
  - Proven able to fail, twice over: mutating `subscription.go:355` to `retryEnd := time.Now()` fails on the ordering assertion (`Expected '59.029097ms' to be greater than or equal to '1s'`), not the timeout branch — a 17x margin, up from 3.2x at the old budget; and removing the dead-port redirect fails on `So(closed, ShouldBeTrue)`. Both halves of the Convey's intent are live. Green under load (8 busy loops on 4 CPUs) across 30+ iterations, plus `-race` clean.
